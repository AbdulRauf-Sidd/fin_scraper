&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"&gt;Dear fellow Coca-Cola shareholders,&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"&gt;We write in reference to the Board&amp;rsquo;s brief
statement opposing our food waste shareholder proposal&amp;mdash;Item 5 in its proxy statement. Below is the entire opposition statement (in
bold, and separated into sections), followed by our responses. We&amp;rsquo;re confident you&amp;rsquo;ll agree that if anything, the Board&amp;rsquo;s
statement only further justifies adoption of our proposal.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;Food waste is a global
challenge, and we all have a part to play in reducing it.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;Yet, as our proposal points out,
Coca-Cola&amp;rsquo;s latest Business &amp;amp; Sustainability Report never once addresses, nor even references, food waste. Neither does its
latest Environmental Update (published August 2024).&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;For The Coca-Cola Company&amp;rsquo;s
global system generally, our operations result in little food waste. It is important to note that our Company purchases processed ingredients
from industrial suppliers which are used in our beverages and sold in shelf stable formats, such as orange juice, apple juice and refined
sugar which, again, results in little food waste.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify; color: #333333"&gt;Pointing to parts
of its business that may not be as vulnerable to food waste impacts just distracts from the need to address those that are. Such diversion
tactics should not be countenanced.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;The proponent states that
food waste is especially relevant for a business like Costa Coffee (&amp;ldquo;Costa&amp;rdquo;). The Coca-Cola Company owns Costa, which has
over 4,000 company and franchise operated caf&amp;eacute;s and approximately 16,000 self-serve coffee bars worldwide.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;To be clear, the proposal doesn&amp;rsquo;t
state that food waste is relevant for a business &amp;ldquo;like&amp;rdquo; Costa Coffee; it states that food waste &lt;I&gt;is &lt;/I&gt;relevant for Costa
Coffee. And in our view, owning a caf&amp;eacute; brand with 20,000 locations indeed makes food waste highly relevant to Coca-Cola.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;In Costa&amp;rsquo;s UK company-operated
stores, waste data is recorded in detail...&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;If Costa is already measuring waste
in detail, then simply &lt;I&gt;disclosing&lt;/I&gt; food waste measurements&amp;mdash;as Starbucks does&amp;mdash;should not be at all overly burdensome.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;&lt;/B&gt;...&lt;B&gt;and monitored
against internal goals.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;In addition to its request that
Coca-Cola disclose food waste measurements, our proposal also asks it to establish reduction goals. So, if one of the company&amp;rsquo;s
brands (Costa) already &lt;I&gt;has&lt;/I&gt; goals, then simply disclosing them should also pose no great burden&amp;mdash;and doing so could indeed
implement that aspect of our proposal.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-right: 34.2pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: left"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;!-- Field: Page; Sequence: 2 --&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-right: 34.2pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: left"&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-right: 34.2pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: left"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;To help reduce food waste,
Costa has deployed a number of interventions, including optimizing product ranges, improving forecasting and stock management, and discounting
goods with shorter shelf lives. Where possible, surplus food and coffee ground waste is also collected and sent to facilities who give
waste a second life, for example, through anaerobic digestion or production of biomass fuels. For a number of years, Costa has been engaged
in industry collaborations, including efforts to divert surplus edible food waste.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;In our view, the more a company
invests in food waste reduction, the more shareholders deserve &lt;I&gt;the&lt;/I&gt; fundamental data needed to understand the return on that investment&amp;mdash;which
is precisely what our proposal requests. Indeed, that Costa &amp;ldquo;has deployed a number of interventions&amp;rdquo; to reduce food waste
clearly shows that food waste is relevant to the company and is even more reason to disclose food waste measurements. Otherwise, shareholders
have no measure of if/how those interventions are working.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;As an example, in 2023,
Costa saved over 800,000 bags of food through its work with a UK surplus food rescue redistribution organization.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;This is the only food waste measurement
the company provides, but it&amp;rsquo;s in a unit (&amp;ldquo;bags&amp;rdquo;) that shareholders have no way to understand. Further, the company
only gives the number of bags of food waste &lt;I&gt;donated&lt;/I&gt;&amp;mdash;not the number &lt;I&gt;generated&lt;/I&gt;. Thus, there is no way for shareholders
to know what percentage of Costa&amp;rsquo;s total food waste these 800,000 bags represent.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;Moreover, for all its references
to the other parts of its operations where food waste may not be so problematic&amp;mdash;and for all its references to how Costa is working
to reduce food waste&amp;mdash;there were still at least 800,000 bags of surplus food in one year alone. And we don&amp;rsquo;t know&amp;mdash;because
the company isn&amp;rsquo;t disclosing&amp;mdash;how much more food waste is generated.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;That&amp;rsquo;s the information shareholders
should be informed about though. Coca-Cola shouldn&amp;rsquo;t distract shareholders with the shelf-stable aspects of its operations, but
on those aspects that generate hundreds of thousands (perhaps even millions, when totaled) of &amp;ldquo;bags&amp;rdquo; of food waste each year.
Instead, it should meaningfully report on the amounts of food waste generated, the amounts it&amp;rsquo;s able to divert from landfills, and
its targets for reducing that waste.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;At The Coca-Cola Company
we focus our efforts where we can have the greatest impact, specifically at the intersection of our sustainability priorities of water,
packaging and climate.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;We refer shareholders to (1) the
opposition statement&amp;rsquo;s opening line, in which Coca-Cola acknowledges it must play a part in reducing food waste, and (2) the opposition
statement&amp;rsquo;s content regarding Costa Coffee&amp;rsquo;s focus on food waste&amp;mdash;including that, apparently, it already measures waste
and has goals for reducing it. These statements show that food waste is &lt;I&gt;clearly&lt;/I&gt; significant to the company&amp;rsquo;s business.&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;ldquo;We are committed to reporting
on these areas in the future along with fulfilling the requirements of relevant regulatory schemes.&amp;rdquo;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;&lt;B&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/B&gt;&lt;/P&gt;

&lt;P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 34.2pt 0pt 27pt; text-align: justify"&gt;While we appreciate the company&amp;rsquo;s
pledge to report on water, packaging, and climate, our proposal calls for action on a distinct and critical issue: &lt;I&gt;food waste&lt;/I&gt;.
On that crucial issue, as our proposal points out, for years, Costa&amp;rsquo;s website has claimed that it&amp;rsquo;s &amp;ldquo;striving to get
better data on exactly how much food gets wasted across our stores, so that we can put in place interim targets to achieve our ultimate
aspiration that &amp;lsquo;no edible food should go to waste&amp;rsquo;.&amp;rdquo; Yet still, the company discloses neither its total food waste,
nor measurable targets.&lt;/P&gt;
